HAKUTO APPS & MODS - ALL IN ONE VERSION
------------------------------------------------
Features:
- Individual APK downloads: /apks/*.apk
- Mods download: /mods/Left 4 dead 2 mods.zip
- All-in-One button: Uses JSZip in browser to bundle all files into Hakuto-Complete-Pack.zip

Setup:
1. Delete placeholder files in apks/ and put your 19 real APKs (same filenames)
2. Replace mods/Left 4 dead 2 mods.zip with your real mod zip
3. Drag folder to https://app.netlify.com/drop

The All-in-One button will fetch all your real files and create one big zip for users.
